## Full description
Fill up for less. PumpWatch shows petrol and diesel prices at filling stations near you across Ireland, so you can see the cheapest pump before you drive.

HOW IT WORKS
• Open the map to see prices around you. The cheapest is marked in green.
• Switch between petrol and diesel.
• Sort stations by price, distance or the newest price.
• Spotted a price? Tap "Report a price", snap the sign, and PumpWatch reads the numbers for you. Check them and submit in seconds.
• Get directions to any station in one tap.
• Set a low-price alert so you see when fuel nearby drops below your target.

BUILT BY DRIVERS
Every price comes from a driver who passed the station. The more people use PumpWatch, the better it gets in your area.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No sign-up, no ads, no tracking.
• Photos are read on your phone and never uploaded.

Prices are shared by users and may be out of date, so always check the pump before filling up.
