PumpWatch Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026 · Developer: VIP Tech, Dublin, Ireland

What PumpWatch does

PumpWatch shows petrol and diesel prices at filling stations in Ireland. Prices are shared by drivers who take a photo of a station's price sign in the app.

Information we collect

  • Location. The app uses your phone's location to show nearby stations and to work out which station a price report belongs to. When you submit a price, the station's position (latitude and longitude) is saved with the price. We don't keep a history of where you have been.
  • Price reports. The fuel type, price and the time you submitted it.
  • An anonymous ID. The app signs you in anonymously with Firebase Authentication. This random ID is stored with your reports so we can stop abuse. It is not linked to your name, email or phone number.

Photos

When you photograph a price sign, the text is read on your phone using Google ML Kit. The photo is never uploaded or stored by us.

What we don't collect

No name, email, phone number, contacts, or advertising ID. PumpWatch has no ads and no analytics.

Where data is stored

Prices and stations are stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore) in the europe-west2 (London) region. Google acts as our data processor. Station names, positions and prices are visible to all PumpWatch users.

How long we keep it

Price reports are kept only while they are useful for showing fuel prices. You can ask us to delete your reports at any time.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask to see or delete data linked to your anonymous ID. Uninstalling the app removes the ID from your phone. To ask us to delete your reports, email us with the date and approximate place of your reports.

Children

PumpWatch is not aimed at children under 13.
